About

Helping ambitious founders automate their growth

I started Diffram AI after watching smart founders get buried in manual workflows — copy-pasting data, chasing leads by hand, running ops processes that should've been automated years ago. You hired great people to do meaningful work. They shouldn't be doing data entry.
